# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository in the opencode-dotnet organization: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and reviews.

- Be respectful and stay on topic. Discuss code, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or spam.

The maintainer moderates, and may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and block users who break
these rules. Report a problem to [@Blind-Striker](https://github.com/Blind-Striker) through the
contact details on that profile. A security vulnerability goes through the affected repository's
private advisory process instead.
